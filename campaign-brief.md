# Campaign brief: The Signal Archive

## Creative premise
Core question: **What happens when technology knows you better than you know yourself?** The site is an interactive archive: part film guide, part digital mystery, part discussion starter. The real-world hook is JIA, a humanoid robot publicly reported to feature in the film; the thematic hook is happiness, AI, digital validation, and choice.

## Reported details (verify before official use)
Published coverage has reported director Simple Suni; cast including Anil Shetty, Saachi Bindra, Kunal Kapoor, Meghana Gaonkar, Malavika Avinash, Mac Macha, and humanoid robot JIA; JIA developed by Bengaluru-based Machani Robotics; a reported 20 November 2026 release and 14 October 2026 first-look timing. Verify the final credits, release date, language plans and all details with the makers before publication.

## What is fiction in the demo
The Signal Archive interface, Happiness Protocol, Validation Loop, Prediction Error, quiz profiles, prompts, and all dossier text are original fictional campaign concepts. They are not official movie materials, plot leaks, surveillance claims or real incidents.

## Zero-budget rollout
1. Publish and share a screen recording of the archive instead of a standard announcement poster.
2. Post one human-test question as an Instagram Story or Reel and direct viewers to the site.
3. Explain JIA using approved assets and source links; do not use unlicensed stills.
4. Release one clearly labelled fictional clue at a time and invite theories about themes, not fake real-world events.
5. Near release, replace speculative clues with official trailer, showtimes and ticket links supplied or approved by the distributor.
